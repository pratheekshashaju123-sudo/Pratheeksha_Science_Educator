01 ABOUT THE EDUCATOR

Teaching with purpose.
Learning with wonder.

A science classroom should feel like a place where questions are welcome and discoveries are possible.

I hold a B.Ed. in Natural Science, MSc in Environmental Science and BSc in Zoology, complemented by CTET and K-TET qualifications. From February 2021 to July 2024, I contributed to classroom instruction, curriculum development, student assessment, progress reporting and programme coordination through K-DISC’s Mazhavillu initiative. My teaching competencies include inquiry-based learning, differentiated instruction, CBSE/ICSE/SCERT curriculum alignment, practical and field-based science instruction, classroom engagement, educational technology and AI.
